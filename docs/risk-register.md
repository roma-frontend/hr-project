# Risk Register — Strata Platform

> **Scope:** Top 10 Information Security & Operational Risks for Strata Platform  
> **Standard:** SOC 2 Type II (CC3.1, CC3.2) & GDPR Art. 32  
> **Reviewed:** 2026-09-20 | **Owner:** Security Officer (`security@strata.am`)  
> **Scoring:** Likelihood (1–5) × Impact (1–5) = Risk Score (1–25)

---

## Top 10 Platform Risks & Mitigations

| #      | Risk Description                                                                             | Category             | L   | I   | Score  | Mitigation & Controls in Codebase                                                                                                                                                                             | Owner            | Status        |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | --- | --- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------- |
| **1**  | **Biometric Data Compromise** (Face ID descriptor exposure or lack of explicit user consent) | Privacy / Legal      | 2   | 5   | **10** | Explicit consent required at enrollment (`grantBiometricConsent`); face descriptors deleted automatically on offboarding (`completeProgram`); no raw photos stored unencrypted; consent withdrawal supported. | Security Officer | **Mitigated** |
| **2**  | **Superadmin Impersonation Abuse** (Operator snooping on tenant payroll/messages)            | Privileged Access    | 2   | 5   | **10** | Impersonation is reason-tracked, session-bounded (`impersonationSessions`), and logged to immutable `auditLogs`; quarterly access reviews via `exportSuperadminsForAccessReview`.                             | Tech Lead        | **Mitigated** |
| **3**  | **Webhook Secret Leakage & Forgery** (Tampered PSP payment callbacks or attendance logs)     | Application Security | 2   | 4   | **8**  | HMAC-SHA256 signature verification with timing-safe comparison (`safeEqual`); idempotent ledger recording (`localPayments`, `webhookEvents`); IP whitelisting for PSP callbacks.                              | Backend Eng      | **Mitigated** |
| **4**  | **AI Data Egress / Leakage** (Sensitive HR data sent to LLM providers for model training)    | Compliance / AI      | 2   | 4   | **8**  | AI governance module (`convex/aiGovernance.ts`); strict provider terms verifying zero-retention / no training on customer inputs; prompt token sanitation.                                                    | AI Lead          | **Mitigated** |
| **5**  | **Subprocessor / Cloud Outage** (Convex Cloud or Vercel downtime rendering platform offline) | Availability         | 3   | 3   | **9**  | Multi-region CDN caching via Vercel; `/api/health` monitoring with 503 circuit breaking; scheduled enterprise backups (`convex/backups.ts`); RTO 4h / RPO 24h targets.                                        | DevOps           | **Mitigated** |
| **6**  | **Credential Stuffing & Account Takeover**                                                   | Authentication       | 3   | 3   | **9**  | Progressive lockout after failed attempts (`loginAttempts`); WebAuthn Passkeys & TOTP MFA; bcrypt with 12 rounds; adaptive auth risk scoring.                                                                 | Security Eng     | **Mitigated** |
| **7**  | **Tenant Data Isolation Breach** (Cross-tenant query bug exposing another org's data)        | Multi-Tenancy        | 1   | 5   | **5**  | Server-side tenant assertion (`getAuthCaller`, `assertModuleAccess`, `orgId` filtering on all indexes); CI integration tests covering tenant boundary isolation.                                              | Core Eng         | **Mitigated** |
| **8**  | **Payroll File & Banking Tampering** (Malicious alteration of bank transfer records)         | Financial Fraud      | 1   | 5   | **5**  | In-memory stream generation for bank files (`paymentRegister.ts`); segregation of duties (approver cannot be submitter); immutable payroll run snapshots.                                                     | FinTech Eng      | **Mitigated** |
| **9**  | **Unpatched Dependency Vulnerability** (Supply chain attack via NPM)                         | Supply Chain         | 3   | 3   | **9**  | Automated CodeQL SAST and GitHub Dependabot in CI; `npm audit` gate in CI; pinned overrides in `package.json`.                                                                                                | DevOps           | **Mitigated** |
| **10** | **Key Employee Deprovisioning Delay** (Former developer retaining cloud console access)      | Insider Threat       | 2   | 4   | **8**  | Enforced JML Runbook (`docs/runbooks/joiner-mover-leaver.md`) with 24h SLA for voluntary and 1h for involuntary leavers; quarterly access reviews.                                                            | Security Officer | **Mitigated** |

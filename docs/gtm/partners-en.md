## 1. What Strata is

An HR platform for companies in Armenia and the CIS: employee records, biometric and
face-recognition attendance, leave, shift scheduling, payroll with Armenian Tax Service
(SRC) reporting, local payments (Idram/ArCa), hiring, onboarding, performance, learning,
documents with e-signatures, and built-in chat and video meetings — one system, localized
in Armenian, Russian, English and German.

For your clients this usually means replacing some combination of: spreadsheets,
Telegram attendance groups, a task messenger, a separate e-signature tool, and manual
payroll preparation.

## 2. Which clients fit

**A good fit:**

- Armenian companies of 20–300 people with office or shift/field staff.
- Armenian offices of international groups that need the local statutory layer.
- Diaspora businesses that want an Armenian- or Russian-language interface for staff.
- Companies paying salaries through local banks, using Armsoft or 1C for accounting.

**Not a fit (be honest, it saves everyone time):**

- Companies needing payroll in 10+ countries — that is an employer-of-record product.
- Companies whose procurement requires a SOC 2 Type II certificate today.
- US-headquartered companies whose core need is US payroll and device management.

## 4. The five-minute client pitch

1. **"Your attendance is in a Telegram group and your leave is in a spreadsheet."**
   Strata replaces both — with face-recognition kiosks if the client needs them.
2. **"Your payroll person prepares the SRC filing by hand."** Strata produces the
   SRC-ready export (income tax, funded pension, military stamp duty, health insurance,
   ՀՎՀՀ) as a tested, formatted sheet.
3. **"Your staff reads Armenian or Russian."** The interface and support are native, not
   machine-translated — key parity across all four languages is machine-checked on every
   release.
4. **"You pay for four tools and copy data between them."** One product, one invoice,
   no double entry — including e-signatures and document templates.
5. **"You can try it before you decide."** Start free, import employees, judge it on your
   own data. No implementation project required for a 20–100 person company.

## 5. Objections you will hear, and the answers

- **"We already use BambooHR / Personio / HiBob."** Good — keep it for the global
  processes. None of them produces an Armenian SRC filing, syncs with Armsoft, or offers
  an Armenian interface. Strata covers the local layer (and usually replaces the
  spreadsheet work around it).
- **"Is it secure?"** Role-based access, audit logs, SSO (SAML/OIDC), SCIM provisioning,
  a security center, and automated SOC 2 evidence collection; the audit itself is in
  progress. Data export and a public API are available on every plan — a client is never
  locked in.
- **"What if you disappear?"** The same answer as above: full data export, API access,
  Armenian legal entity. Plus: our pricing is per employee, published, and cheaper than
  the sum of tools we replace.
- **"We only need attendance."** Start with the time module. Most clients add leave and
  documents within a quarter because the data is already there.

## 6. How the commercial relationship works

1. **Intro call** — we align on your client base and the partner type that fits.
2. **Demo tenant + training** — your team gets a workspace and a walkthrough.
3. **You introduce, we close** — or you run the whole cycle yourself; either works.
4. **Commission** — recurring share on referred subscriptions, paid monthly; documented
   in the partner agreement.
5. **Support split** — first-line support stays with the client; you never become our
   helpdesk.
